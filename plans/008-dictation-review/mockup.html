A starting point

What survives the first week?

A good first impression is easy. The useful details tend to arrive later, in the ordinary moments when you stop paying attention to the product itself.

For this review, start with one of those moments. What did you keep noticing? What stopped bothering you? What would you tell someone deciding whether to buy it?

The firsthand detail belongs here.

The proposed flow

One message. Type it or say it.

01 / DICTATE

Words land where you write

Dictate becomes Stop dictation. Speech appends to your message, and you can keep editing.

02 / CLEAN UP · OPTIONAL

A light proofread

Remove ums and uhs, and tidy punctuation. Keep your meaning and details. Undo if you prefer the original.

03 / SEND

Send when you’re ready

Send stops dictation and uses the text you can see. Nothing arriving later can change that message.
